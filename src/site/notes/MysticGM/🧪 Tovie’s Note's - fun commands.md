# 🎉 MysticGM – Fun Deck

_Memes, GIFs, jokes, and party tricks to keep chats buzzing._

---

## ✨ What is this?
- Memes, GIFs, quotes, pickup lines, and jokes.
- Light games/polls: WYR, truth-or-dare, choose, rate, vibecheck.
- Text transformers (uwu, emojify).

## 📚 Contents (pick your spark)
- **Drops:** `meme`, `gif`, `quote`, `pickup-line`, `dadjoke`
- **Games:** `wyr`, `truth-or-dare`, `choose`, `rate`, `vibecheck`, `roll`, `8ball`
- **Transforms:** `uwu`, `emojify`

## 🧭 How to Navigate
- Use `meme`/`gif` for instant vibes.
- Pair `choose` + `vibecheck` to resolve debates with flair.
- Keep `uwu`/`emojify` for fun copy tweaks.

## 🪄 Command Grimoire — `/fun ...`
- `8ball` — Ask the magic 8-ball a question.
- `roll` — Roll dice.
- `meme` — Get a random meme.
- `gif` — Send a fitting reaction GIF.
- `ship` — Check compatibility between two users.
- `roast` — Send a light, opt-in roast.
- `compliment` — Send a wholesome compliment.
- `pickup-line` — Generate a cheesy pickup line.
- `dadjoke` — Serve a groan-worthy dad joke.
- `uwu` — Uwuify a message.
- `emojify` — Translate words into emojis.
- `quote` — Get an inspirational or quirky quote.
- `wyr` — Get a “Would You Rather” question.
- `truth-or-dare` — Safe truth or dare prompt.
- `vibecheck` — Rate the vibes of anything 0-100.
- `choose` — Let the bot choose between options.
- `rate` — Rate something out of 10.
